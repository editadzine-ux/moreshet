# Homepage timeline — 18 October 2026

The editorial focus is community life, recovery and transmission of heritage.
The Holocaust remains explicit in the 1943 entry, with a restrained heading
and no dramatic statistics. The 1945 entry introduces rebuilding and migration
over the following years. The new entries connect that recovery to ongoing
heritage work in Greece and Israel; they do not claim institutional succession
between the museums and the Petah Tikva center.

## Sources for the added milestones

- **1956 — Leon Recanati home, Petah Tikva.** The Recanati family history gives
  1956 as its founding year: https://danymatyrecanati.co.il/about/family
  Academic context identifies the home with aging Balkan immigrants:
  https://efrata.emef.ac.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Pages-from-%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%97%D7%A7-%D7%A8%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%98%D7%99.pdf
- **1977 — Jewish Museum of Greece, Athens.** Its own history dates the museum
  to 1977 and describes collecting objects and documents from Greek Jewish
  communities: https://jewishmuseum.gr/en/history/
- **2001 — Jewish Museum of Thessaloniki.** Its building/history page states
  that the museum opened in 2001 and describes its community history and
  everyday-life displays:
  https://www.jmth.gr/article-05032014-to-ktirio-tou-mouseiou
- **2010 — heritage center operating in Petah Tikva.** Retains the year already
  present in the site's About and Founder pages. Contemporary first-person
  reports in *Kol Hanitzolim*, the Greek survivors' association newsletter,
  describe visiting the heritage center at the Recanati home on 6 September
  2010 and a center-organized event on that date. The timeline deliberately
  describes activity in 2010 rather than inventing an exact inauguration date.
  Reproduced newsletter:
  https://abcdocz.com/doc/2631736/קול-הניצולים---ארגון-ניצולי-מחנות-ההשמדה-יוצאי-יוון-בישראל
  The association's later legal registration is distinct from the start of
  activity. The same newsletter describes postwar migration and the joint
  effort to establish the center.
- **Today — education and culture.** Existing program categories, consistent
  with the center's official site: https://www.salonikaheritage.com/

The 1492, 1520 and 1917 dates were already present. The 1492 and 1917 prose has
been softened without removing the expulsion from Spain or the great fire.
The museum's history provides broader context:
https://www.jmth.gr/article-20032014-oi-evraioi-tis-thessalonikis

## Event carousel

Two existing event cards appear together above 700px. Both retain their image,
white ticket, perforation, badge and CTA. From 701px to 1100px each ticket's
image sits above its text. On narrow screens one readable card and a preview
of the next are shown. Controls are generated from the real item count and
hidden when all items fit; no extra events, dates or duplicate cards were added.

Follow-up: at the user's request, the slider now has six distinct cards. Four
new cards reuse established center activities (heritage food, guided visits,
holidays and the 360-degree experience), their existing images and inquiry
links. Their badge says "פעילות המרכז" and their CTA asks for details and
coordination. No unconfirmed dates, speakers or ticket availability are stated.
The decorative cursor orb and its tracking loop have also been removed.
